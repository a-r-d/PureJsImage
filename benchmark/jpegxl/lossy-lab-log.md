## Compression plateau

The compression trials since E3 have not produced a retained 1% development
BD-rate improvement. The speed improvements in LL-036 and LL-044 remain valuable,
but do not close either Butteraugli target. This reaches the stop condition in
section 9 of [LOSSY_STEERING.md](LOSSY_STEERING.md). No further compression trial
is running. The retained working changes and all measured evidence are preserved.

The remaining approaches are ranked by their likely usefulness, without assigning
an unmeasured percentage gain:

1. Diagnose im26-3000's repeated final-quality regression using the held shared
   reconstruction rows. Local selector error has repeatedly failed to protect this
   development image. This has the strongest evidence for explaining the failures.
2. Test one precision-refinement pass against regional error in final reconstructed
   pixels. This could address the broader Butteraugli gap, but requires a model
   that protects every color channel and fits the effort-7 time budget.
3. Replace expensive complete-candidate searches on originals with cheaper
   selection. This addresses the unqualified 5x original speed target; the palette
   precheck only applies at up to 1 MP and cannot solve that target.

Holdout, watch-original qualification, successful full checks on the resumed
source, and a commit remain pending. The resumed full check fails as recorded
below. E1/E2 still have protected-image regressions against the unchanged
baseline, so the combined working configuration is not promoted.
