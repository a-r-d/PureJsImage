# AVIF high-bit post-filter decode evidence (2026-08-09)

## Scope

This result expands the pinned high-bit AVIF decode boundary with six lossy single-frame fixtures:

- 10-bit YUV 4:2:0 with deblocking, CDEF, and Wiener restoration;
- 10-bit YUV 4:2:2 with CDEF and Wiener restoration;
- limited-range 10-bit YUV 4:2:0 with deblocking, CDEF, and self-guided restoration;
- 12-bit YUV 4:2:0, 4:2:2, and 4:4:4 with deblocking and CDEF.

The existing TypeScript reconstruction and post-filter kernels required no new branch: the new fixtures prove that their depth-scaled arithmetic already matches independent native decoders for these syntax combinations.

## Fixture provenance

`filtered-lossy-10bpc-yuv420-192x128.avif`, `filtered-lossy-10bpc-yuv422-64x64.avif`, and the three 12-bit fixtures use deterministic high-frequency Y4M sources generated by `prepare-high-bit-expanded-fixtures.ts`. `self-guided-10bpc-yuv420-320x192.avif` uses a deterministic 320x192 crop of the checksum-pinned `fox.profile0.8bpc.yuv420.avif` corpus image, converted to limited-range 10-bit YUV 4:2:0.

All six AVIF files were encoded with libavif `avifenc` 1.3.0 and libaom 3.12.1, one encoder job, and checksum-pinned encoder settings. Source and output checksums are part of `high-bit-expanded-fixtures.ts`.

The large subsampled displayed-RGB deltas are color-conversion and chroma-upsampling differences after the independently verified native YUV planes. They are recorded, not presented as a quality threshold.

## Boundary

Evidence covers only the six pinned frame-header, entropy, reconstruction, and filter combinations above. It does not claim every legal high-depth AV1 post-filter combination. In particular, self-guided 10-bit YUV 4:4:4 and 12-bit Wiener or self-guided restoration remain unvalidated. High bit depth does not imply PQ, HLG, HDR output, or wide-gamut conversion support. The decoder still emits 8-bit RGBA after retaining native-depth YUV through reconstruction and filtering.
